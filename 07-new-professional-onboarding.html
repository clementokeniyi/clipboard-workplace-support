Hi Aisha, welcome — and great question. You are in the right place, and we will go step by step. There is no rush, and you can ask me at any point.

  1. Check your profile: open the app and confirm your profile shows as verified.
  2. Browse shifts: choose your date and area, then look through the shifts listed.
  3. Open a shift: tap a shift to see the time, location, and role details.
  4. Book it: if it fits, confirm the booking in the app. You will see it in your upcoming shifts.
  5. On the day: check in through the app when you arrive, and check out when you finish.

Try step 1 and 2 when you are ready, and tell me what you see. If a shift list looks empty, try a different date — that is normal at first.

You have got this,
Priya Sharma
